m1well.com is where I point people when a CV is not enough. My experience, an
honest breakdown of what I can do, the projects I build in my own time, and this
blog, all in one place. Call it a digital handshake.

## Who I am

Fullstack developer, over ten years of it, mostly on enterprise applications.
Java and Spring Boot on the backend, Angular on the frontend. Around that MySQL
and Postgres, RabbitMQ for messaging, and Docker, Kubernetes and Jenkins for
everything that has to run somewhere. The page lists those technologies, but
what I wanted it to show is how I work: pragmatic, clean code, domain-driven
design, agile where it actually helps.

## Why a page at all

A CV is a list. This is the longer version, for a potential employer, someone I
might end up working with, or whoever lands here from GitHub. The skills section
has proficiency levels, which is my attempt at being honest instead of listing
every buzzword I have touched once.

### What is on it

The projects are the part I care about most: `MaverickWave`, the CSS framework
this site runs on, `Idently` and `versions`. They are the reason the GitHub and
Dev.to links sit in the header. Coding does not stop for me at 5pm, and the
repos say that better than a sentence about passion would.

The page itself is built with Astro, which I picked because I wanted to learn
it. Static output, component-based, TypeScript, and almost nothing shipped to
the browser that does not need to be there.

The layout, the fact that it runs on my own framework, the disclaimers about
privacy and about this being my private page and not my employer's: all of that
is deliberate.
